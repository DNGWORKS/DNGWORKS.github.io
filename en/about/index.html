About

Marketing does not start with advertising.

It starts with the product, the experience, trust, and the ability to turn data into better decisions.

DNGWORKS is the consulting practice of Dũng Nguyễn, working from Đà Nẵng with small and medium businesses in Vietnam. Most projects begin at the same place: the company has done plenty, but nobody can say which part is producing revenue.

The method is diagnosis before proposal. In practice the first sessions go into reading the data that already exists, listening to the sales team explain why deals were lost, and following the real customer journey rather than a funnel diagram on paper.

After diagnosis the work turns to system design: positioning and messaging, content and touchpoints, the conversion flow, the data layer, then automation of whatever repeats. A project is not finished when files are handed over. It is finished when the internal team can run the system without me.

Working principles

Diagnose first. Design the system second.

  • Diagnose before quoting

    The right scope only appears after the problem is understood. Quoting before diagnosis sells a solution to an unknown problem.

  • Every number traces to a source

    Any metric in a report has to say where it came from, how it was measured and where the error sits. A number you cannot trace is not a number you decide with.

  • Hand over so the business can run it

    A system that depends on the person who built it is unfinished. Process, documentation and training are in scope, not extras.
