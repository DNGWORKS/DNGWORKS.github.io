Diagnosis

Start with the bottleneck, not the tool.

A good-looking website, an ad campaign or an AI workflow is only worth something when it serves the actual business problem and leaves the company able to operate better.

Positioning is unclear

Customers cannot say why they should choose you over the alternative. Every media cost after that pays for the ambiguity.

Price is the only reason customers mention

Marketing runs in pieces

Content, advertising, the website, sales and data all run in parallel but never connect, so nobody sees the whole picture.

Each channel reports its own number and they never reconcile

Growth cannot be measured

There is plenty of activity but no tracking, no dashboard and no decision standard, so optimisation runs on instinct.

No answer to which spend produced revenue

Method

Diagnose first. Design the system second.

I do not sell a fixed package before understanding the problem. Scope is built from real priorities, resources and risk.
