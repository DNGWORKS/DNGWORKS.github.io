Where should AI enter a marketing workflow first?

Do not begin by buying many tools. Begin with repetitive, time-consuming tasks that have clear inputs.

Author
Dũng Nguyễn
Published
Jun 02, 2026
4 min read

Đồ hoạ biên tập DNGWORKS

AI is most valuable when it removes friction from a clearly defined process. Early research, brief standardisation, lead classification, reporting and content repurposing are practical starting points.

Implementation rule

Select one workflow, define inputs and outputs, set quality checks and only then automate. Do not automate chaos.
