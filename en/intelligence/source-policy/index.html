Source policy

How DNG Intelligence selects stories, handles rights and displays time.

Three content types, never mixed

Industry news is third-party content: the original headline, the source, the publication time and a short excerpt, with a link to the original. A DNG briefing is a short piece written by DNGWORKS from several cited sources. Analysis and playbooks are longer original work with method and examples. The three are always labelled separately and never presented as one another.

No full-text copying

DNGWORKS does not store, re-translate or republish anyone else's article in full. Excerpts are limited to what a reader needs in order to decide whether to open the original.

Timestamps say what they are

The publication time of the original and the time the system fetched the data are two different numbers and are displayed separately. When a source provides no publication time, the page says the date is unknown rather than guessing.

Images

Images are only used where the source permits reuse. Where no usable image exists, the page uses DNGWORKS editorial artwork and labels it as an illustration rather than a photograph of the event.

Filtered on relevance, not on traffic

Stories are scored on relevance to business, marketing, AI, technology and e-commerce. Entertainment, sport and other off-industry topics are excluded even when a source is pushing them hard.

When a source fails

The system keeps the most recent valid snapshot and states when it last synchronised, instead of showing an empty list or inventing replacement stories.
