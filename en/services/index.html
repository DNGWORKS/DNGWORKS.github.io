Data sits in several places and nobody uses it to decide anything. Reports exist to be filed, not to change course.

Diagnosis

Before building a dashboard, you need to know which decisions the business actually makes, how often, and which number is missing to make them less of a guess.

Method

Define the data set that must exist, standardise how it is measured, build the reporting layer around decisions, then put AI on the repeatable steps: summarising, classifying and alerting.

Delivered

  • Data map and shared metric definitions
  • GA4, GTM and pixel setup with data validation
  • Dashboards organised by decision, not by channel
  • AI workflows for research, synthesis and alerting

Positioning is not a slogan. It answers who you serve, which problem you solve distinctly better, and what evidence proves it.

Research customers and competitors, settle the insight and USP, build the message architecture, then package it into a visual system and sales assets usable day to day.

Growth Marketing

  • Demand
  • Conversion
  • Retention
  • Meta Ads
  • Google Ads
  • SEO
  • WordPress

Ad budget goes up and revenue does not follow, because the funnel leaks at steps nobody tracks.

Diagnosis

Advertising amplifies a system, including its weaknesses. Find the step that loses the most people before spending more.

Method

Build landing pages and the conversion flow, set up tracking you can trust, run campaigns as testable hypotheses, then optimise on a fixed cycle.

Delivered

  • Landing pages and conversion flow
  • Meta Ads and Google Ads campaign structure
  • An experiment system with evaluation standards
  • Growth dashboard and optimisation cycle

Marketing Operations

  • System
  • Automation
  • Scale
  • n8n
  • CRM
  • Google Workspace
  • Looker Studio

Everything depends on a few people remembering. When they are away, quality drops.

Diagnosis

Weak operations is rarely a lack of effort. It is the absence of process that can be written down, handed over and checked.

Method

Standardise process and work states, build a management dashboard, automate the repeatable steps, then train and hand over to the internal team.

Delivered

  • Marketing process and CRM or lead workflow
  • Response SLA and handover standard between marketing and sales
  • Automation of repeatable tasks with n8n and scripts
  • Management dashboard, training and team handover
